## Moment 01: The Monolith Gateway Reveal (Prologue Entry)

- **Trigger**: Initial page load & dismissal of the loading screen.
- **Camera**: Positioned at `[0, 1.2, 4.0]` looking down the center of the corridor; performs a subtle forward dolly `[0, 1.2, 3.2]` with low-angle upward tilt.
- **Environment**: Vast subterranean graphite corridor extending 160m; monolithic pillars flank the space; obsidian fog with sightlines to distant chambers.
- **Light**: A single focused overhead tungsten beam (`#F0C98B`) casts a long shadow along the central brass rail; distant copper glow visible 46m ahead.
- **Object**: Central brass guide rail reflects tungsten light; monolithic portal columns establish monumental scale.
- **DOM**: Kinetic typography reveal: `Yogeshwaran` and `Muthuraman` emerge through word-mask clip-paths; 4-metric foundational ledger renders with hairline dividers.
- **Motion**: Word masks translate $115\% \rightarrow 0\%$ with smooth cubic bezier; telemetry ticker initializes from `00` to values (`9.37`, `1`, `13`, `7`).
- **Transition**: Smooth deceleration into rest; subtle ambient breathing in particles.
- **Purpose**: Establishes that the visitor has entered a physical, monumental subterranean world of verification, not a flat web page.
