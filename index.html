Chapter two — The research

Teaching a machine to recognise a lie.

Thousands of job seekers apply to postings that were never real. The question I took to my final-year research was whether a model could flag the fraud before the application goes out — and, just as importantly, explain enough of its reasoning to be trusted.

Published Peer reviewed Oral presentation ICISD26-408

Fake Job Posting and Online Recruitment Scam Detection Utilizing Machine Learning and Advanced Natural Language Processing Techniques

International Conference on Intelligent Systems and Digital Transformation (ICISD'26) SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Vadapalani Campus, Chennai · 6–7 April 2026

The paper proposes a hybrid neuro-symbolic detection system. DistilBERT supplies contextual embeddings of the posting text, an Isolation Forest catches anomalies the supervised model has never seen, and a stacking ensemble of classifiers makes the final call. The unsupervised layer is what lets it flag zero-day scams rather than only the patterns it was trained on.

The central difficulty is class imbalance: in the EMSCAD dataset genuine and fraudulent postings run at roughly 95:5, so a model that simply answers "genuine" every time already scores 95%. Handling that imbalance honestly is most of the work, which is why F1 and ROC-AUC matter here far more than raw accuracy.

github.com/Yoge-2004/digital-diary-application

Digital Diary

A private-by-default journal, built deliberately without a front-end framework: FastAPI, Jinja2 templates and plain JavaScript, with SQLAlchemy over SQLite or Postgres. Auth uses JWT access and refresh tokens held in httponly cookies rather than local storage.

Entries carry a mood, tags and an optional location, and can each be favourited, pinned, archived or bookmarked independently. Sharing is per-entry — grant one specific user access or mint a public link, either with an expiry. Attachments render inline when they are images. The dashboard tracks totals, mood distribution and current and longest writing streaks, and every page has a matching JSON REST API under /api.
