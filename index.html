Chapter Five

The Discovery

Research that matters.

Conference PaperICISD26-408

Fake Job Posting and Online Recruitment Scam Detection Utilizing Machine Learning and Advanced Natural Language Processing Techniques

SRM Institute of Science and Technology · ICISD'26 · April 6–7, 2026

Abstract

This research proposes a hybrid neuro-symbolic fraud detection system combining DistilBERT contextual embeddings, Isolation Forest anomaly detection and a stacking ensemble to identify fraudulent job postings.

The study addresses class imbalance in the EMSCAD dataset and reports 99% accuracy while targeting zero-day scams through unsupervised anomaly detection.
