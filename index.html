01 · Research

Where the fraud detector fails.

A fraud detector for job postings: DistilBERT embeddings, an Isolation Forest and a stacking ensemble. The chart is its confusion matrix on postings it did not see in training. Drag to turn it. Each column is what the model said; each row is what the posting really was. Taller blocks hold more postings; heights use a square-root scale so small errors stay visible.

Accuracy
Frauds missed
Real postings flagged

Source: results/evaluation_report.txt in the fraud-detection repository, BERT Fusion model. Test split: 6,816 postings (3,413 fraud, 3,403 real). Training split: 8,520 postings.

The repository trains on a balanced 50/50 dataset. A classifier that always answers “real” scores about 50% here, not 95%. The 95:5 ratio in the paper describes the original EMSCAD data, not this split.

Paper: “Fake Job Posting and Online Recruitment Scam Detection Utilizing Machine Learning and Advanced Natural Language Processing Techniques”, ICISD’26, SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Vadapalani, 6–7 April 2026 (ICISD26-408).
